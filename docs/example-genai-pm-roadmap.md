## Overview

You will be able to identify key generative AI concepts and relevant use cases for product management, develop a working prototype of an AI-enhanced product feature, create a detailed launch plan for a production-ready AI feature, and formulate a strategic proposal for AI-driven product innovation. The roadmap progresses from foundational knowledge through practical building and professional deployment to strategic mastery. It is designed for product managers who want to integrate generative AI into their product development process with a clear, structured approach.

## Getting started

1. Set aside your first session for 2 to 3 hours to focus on Phase 1: Foundations.
2. Ensure you have access to the required software tools and platforms specified in Phase 1, such as AI concept mapping tools and any recommended development environments.
3. Begin by reviewing the Generative AI Concept Map and Use Case Summary to understand the landscape and terminology.
4. Complete the milestone project for Phase 1, which involves mapping out potential AI use cases relevant to your product context.
5. Confirm you can clearly explain key generative AI concepts and identify at least three viable use cases for your product before moving to Phase 2.
6. Once you have completed the milestone project and feel confident with the foundational concepts, you are ready to start building your AI-enhanced product feature prototype in Phase 2.
